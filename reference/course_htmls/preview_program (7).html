Biology, B.S. + Credential

Print Degree Planner (opens a new window) | Print-Friendly Page (opens a new window)

The Biology, BS + Credential combines the requirements for the Biology degree and credential courses within one integrated program that allows students to finish their baccalaureate degree and credential in less time. This Integrated Biology Teacher Education Program (Biology ITEP) is designed for students who desire to become middle and high school Science/Biology teachers.

*Please note that this option requires more units per semester, and requires students to commit to a  prescribed course sequence with additional requirements. Students are encouraged to consult their advisor for details.

In order to graduate, you will also need to complete your General Education and university requirements, some of which are embedded within the major.

Students must maintain a minimum GPA of 2.0 and must earn a C- or above in each course that serves as a pre-requisite to a more advanced course.

Biology-BS Core Requirements ~ 39 units

  • Complete all of the following courses with a grade of C- or above, in order to fulfill pre-requisites for more advanced courses:

  • MATH 150 - Calculus I Units: 4 This course requires prior preparation for calculus (e.g. to a level provided by successful completion of MATH 130)
  • CHEM 110 - Chemistry I Units: 4
  • CHEM 110L - Chemistry I Lab Units: 1
  • CHEM 111 - Chemistry II Units: 4
  • CHEM 111L - Chemistry II Lab Units: 1
  • BIO 210 - Molecular and Cell Biology and Animal Physiology Units: 4
  • BIO 210L - Molecular and Cell Biology and Animal Physiology Lab Units: 1
  • BIO 211 - Ecology, Evolution, Biodiversity and Plants Units: 4
  • BIO 211L - Ecology, Evolution, Biodiversity and Plants Lab Units: 1
  • BIO 301 - Research and Technical Writing in Biology Units: 3

  •  

  • Complete all of the following courses:

  • CHEM 211 - Organic Chemistry I Units: 3
  • CHEM 211L - Organic Chemistry I Lab Units: 1
  • PHYS 220 - Physics I Units: 4
  • BIO 311 - Genetics Units: 4

Biology Teacher Education Concentration ~ 37-45 units

  • Complete one of the following Statistics courses:

  • STAT 100 - Introduction to Statistics Units: 3
  • STAT 250 - Applied Stat:Sci Tech Units: 4

  •  

  • Complete all of the following Teacher Preparation courses:

  • GEOL 210 - Earth Science for Teachers Units: 3
  • PHYS 221 - Physics II Units: 4
  • BIO 320 - Microbiology Units: 4
  • BIO 330 - Vertebrate Physiology Units: 3
  • BIO 340 - Ecology Units: 4
  • BIO 341 - Evolution Bio & Pop Genetics Units: 3

  •  

  • Complete all of the following Teacher Credential courses:

  • SPED 560 - Inclusionary Practices Units: 3
  • ED 605A - Secondary Curriculum & Instruction: Science Units: 3
  • ED 607 - Adolescent Development & Learning Units: 3
  • ED 624 - Stage I Supervised Secondary Teaching Units: 8-12
  • ED 624A - Stage I Supervised Secondary Teaching Seminar Units: 3
  • ED 625 - Stage II Secondary Supervised Teaching Units: 8-12
  • ED 625A - Stage II Supervised Secondary Teaching Seminar Units: 3
  • ED 627 - Secon Lang & Lit Develop Units: 3
  • ED 628 - Secon Pedagogy Ling Diverse Units: 3
  • ED 510 - Providing Behavior Supports in the Classroom Units: 3
  • ED 643 - Educational Measurement and Evaluation Units: 3
