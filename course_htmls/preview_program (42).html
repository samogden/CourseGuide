Humanities & Communication, B.A ~ Historical Studies Concentration

Print Degree Planner (opens a new window) | Print-Friendly Page (opens a new window)
Effective February 19, 2027 Enrollment in the Historical Studies Concentration is Suspended.

Interested students are encouraged to enroll in the History, B.A. [beginning Fall 2027] For more information, please contact the Department of Social Sciences and Global Studies (Building 504-2320; 831-582-3890; ssgs@csumb.edu).

HCOM-BA Core Courses ~ 29-31 units

In order to graduate, you will also need to complete your General Education  and University Requirements .

One course, unless otherwise indicated, must be an approved research or theory intensive course, indicated as (RTI) in the course lists below.

Students may not double count an HCOM core course and a concentration course, nor may they double count a concentration course for a required area within the major core.
